Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Concrete Lift & Level Melbourne West ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information We Collect via Forms and Calls

  • Direct Contact Data: Full name, telephone number, email address, and physical property address.
  • Quote Form Information: Specific details regarding your concrete condition, approximate slab drops, property type (residential/commercial), and project descriptions.
  • Automated Telemetry: Standard connection metadata, including timestamp, IP address, device type, and visit referrer information.

2. Purpose of Collection & Trade Routing

We collect your personal information strictly to respond to inquiries, arrange on-site optical laser elevation inspections, and connect prospective clients directly with licensed, insured trade contractors operating mobile resin-injection units within your specific geographic corridor in Melbourne's West.

3. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertising brokers. Personal information submitted is disclosed exclusively to verified trade partners servicing the City of Wyndham and Western Melbourne for the direct fulfillment of your quote request.

4. Access and Correction Rights

To request access to personal information we hold about you or request corrections, contact our compliance desk at: privacy@concreteliftlevel.com.au.
