Why Structural Polyurethane Outperforms Concrete Replacement

Demolishing settled concrete with jackhammers damages the surrounding sub-base, tears up established turf and garden beds, and leaves areas unusable for up to 4 weeks while new concrete cures. Most critically, pouring a heavy new 32 MPa slab places thousands of kilograms of fresh weight on top of the exact same weak, voided reactive basalt clay sub-soil that caused the initial drop.

Structural resin injection addresses the root engineering problem beneath the concrete. By introducing closed-cell expanding polymers directly into subterranean voids, the soil is consolidated and compacted first. Only once the underlying bearing strata achieves solid resistance does the hydraulic expanding force lift the slab cleanly back into grade.
