Why Driveways Sink at the Garage Threshold

Across Melbourne’s outer Western suburbs, residential driveways routinely drop along the garage entrance line. This occurs because the main house and garage are constructed as an engineered internal foundation, while the external driveway slab is poured over crushed rock backfill.

When reactive basalt clays shrink in summer, gaps form beneath the concrete. Rain runoff channels directly into the joint between the garage and the driveway, eroding the sub-base bedding. The concrete slab drops 20mm to 75mm, catching vehicle spoilers and directing water back toward internal structural framing.

The 4-Stage Injection Process

  1. Laser Survey: Optical levels map the elevation profile and pinpoint exact subterranean void locations.
  2. 16mm Micro-Port Drilling: Small injection holes are drilled into expansion cuts and joints, preserving surface concrete integrity.
  3. Hydrophobic Polymer Expansion: Injected polymer compacts the loose base and expands with high upward force to lift the slab flush.
  4. Port Sealing: Drill holes are patched with color-matched aggregate; the driveway is quickly restored for residential vehicle use.
