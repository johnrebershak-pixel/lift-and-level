Geotechnical Realities: The Quaternary Basalt Plains

The City of Wyndham rests upon the Victorian Western Volcanic Plains. Geologically, this region is characterized by weathered olivine basalt formations that decompose into dense, highly reactive smectite and montmorillonite clays. Under the Australian Standard for Residential Slabs and Footings (AS 2870-2011), these soils are routinely classified as:

  • Class H1 & H2 (Highly Reactive): Significant expected surface soil movement ($y_s$ between 40mm and 75mm).
  • Class E (Extremely Reactive): Deep ground movement exceeding 75mm during prolonged seasonal moisture variations.

These reactive clays behave like a sponge. During hot, dry Melbourne summers, the soil loses moisture, shrinks dramatically, and pulls away from concrete perimeter footings and crossover bedding. During heavy winter rain events, the clay swells unevenly. Over repeated seasonal cycles, uncompacted sub-bases settle, leaving external concrete driveways, footpaths, and garage lintels suspended over empty underground voids.

Common Concrete Failure Points in Wyndham Estate Builds

  • Garage Threshold Separation: Internal garage floors are typically supported by waffle pod foundations, while external driveways rest on uncompacted boundary backfill. The driveway drops 25mm to 75mm below the garage lip, scraping vehicle bumpers.
  • Perimeter Footpath Rotation: Walkways along boundary fences tilt backwards toward brick walls, directing stormwater runoff into brick weep holes rather than toward street stormwater pits.
  • Council Crossover Displacement: Front crossover slabs drop below curb levels, creating dangerous public trip hazards.

Why Non-Invasive Polyurethane Lifting Is the Engineered Solution

Pouring heavy fresh concrete over reactive Wyndham clay simply places thousands of kilograms of new weight on top of the same shifting sub-base, guaranteeing future cracking. Our precision polyurethane injection introduces lightweight, structural expanding polymer directly beneath the concrete. It fills voids, compacts loose clay fill, and levels the slab flush without damaging your garden beds or disturbing underground services.
