Preventing Structural Stormwater Damage

When garage thresholds drop, the natural outward drainage slope is reversed. During wind-driven rain, pooling stormwater seeps beneath garage door weather seals, wetting internal timber framing and brick veneer weep holes. Polyurethane injection restores positive fall away from the building, stopping stepped brick cracking and protecting internal structures.
